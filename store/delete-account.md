# Delete your Nudge account

> Published on the web at `/delete-account`. This is the link Google Play asks
> for ("Delete account URL"). Your details in `[brackets]` are filled in from
> [legal-details.json](legal-details.json) when the page is built.

Nudge is made by [your name]. You can delete your Nudge account and everything in it at any time, with or without the app.

## In the app

1. Open Nudge and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** to confirm.

Your account and all its data are deleted right away. This can't be undone.

## On the web

Sign in to Nudge in your browser with the button at the end of this page, then follow the same steps: **Settings** → **Delete account** → **Delete my account**.

## By email

If you can't sign in, email [contact email] from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Nudge account". Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: email address, login, and your name and picture from Google or Apple if you used them
- Your tasks, habits and habit check-ins, tags and checklists
- Calendar entries you created in Nudge, and your Google Calendar connection
- Your profile picture
- Your settings, such as your daily reminder

## What's kept

- **Backups:** copies kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within [number of days, e.g. 7 days].
- **Feedback** you sent from the app stays on Nudge's public GitHub page. It contains only your message and a random account ID, not your name or email. Email [contact email] if you'd like it removed.
